Dark navy #10151f background, #19212e surfaces, warm gold #d8b779, muted #919aaa text. Serif Chinese titles; sans-serif controls. Thin borders, restrained radii, illustrated role portraits. Header with moon mark, 法官控制台 / 玩家视角 / 游戏规则 navigation, room control. Primary screen: title and four-stage stepper, four-column twelve-seat board, right action panel and chronological log. Mobile: one-column personal identity, private actions and room status; judge board collapses to two columns. Hover lifts, selected gold border, explicit keyboard focus; reduced-motion respected.

Intentional functional extensions: lobby creation/join, role secrecy toggle, judge proxy actor selection, seer result, witch potion selection, hunter interruption, copy player invitation, rules dialog, explicit prototype scope. Default preview has demo players; creating a live room starts empty. Exact role preset is four wolves, seer, witch, hunter, idiot, four villagers (reference decorative role text is superseded by this rule). No guard or cupid in this version. No artificial networking status.

Production art: dedicated six-role generated sheet, CSS background positions. No graphical collision, terrain, sprites or parallax are needed in this turn-based party assistant.
